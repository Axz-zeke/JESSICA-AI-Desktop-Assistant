# Home Audio Acoustics, Room Modes & Bass Traps

Room acoustics have a greater impact on what you hear than speakers or audio interfaces. Untreated rectangular rooms suffer from standing waves, flutter echoes, and severe frequency cancellations.

## Acoustic Phenomena
- **Room Modes:** Resonant frequencies determined by room dimensions (Length, Width, Height) causing massive peaks ($+15	ext{dB}$) and nulls ($-20	ext{dB}$) in the sub-bass and bass spectrum ($< 250	ext{Hz}$).
- **Early Reflections:** Direct sound from speakers reflects off side walls and ceilings, arriving at the listener's ear a few milliseconds later and causing comb filtering.

## Treatment Strategy
1. **Broadband Bass Traps:** Dense porous absorption (rigid mineral wool / Owens Corning 703) placed in room corners where bass pressure accumulates.
2. **First Reflection Absorption:** Absorptive panels placed at mirror points along side walls and ceiling clouds.
3. **Diffusion:** Rear wall diffusers scattering high-frequency reflections without deadening the room completely.
