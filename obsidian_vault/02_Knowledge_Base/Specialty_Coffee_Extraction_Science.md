# Specialty Coffee Extraction Science, TDS & Brew Chemistry

Coffee brewing is the controlled chemical extraction of soluble aromatic compounds, organic acids, lipids, and caffeine from roasted, ground coffee beans using hot water.

## The Extraction Curve
1. **Under-Extracted (< 18% yield):** Sour, salty, lacking sweetness, sharp grassy finish (only sour organic acids have dissolved).
2. **Ideal Extraction (18% - 22% yield):** Sweet, complex fruit acidity, rounded body, lingering pleasant aftertaste.
3. **Over-Extracted (> 22% yield):** Bitter, astringent, dry, hollow finish (heavier bitter polyphenols and astringent tannins have dissolved).

## Key Brewing Variables
- **Brew Ratio:** 1:15 to 1:17 (e.g., 15g coffee to 250g water).
- **Water Temperature:** 92°C to 96°C (too hot extracts harsh tannins; too cool fails to extract sweet sugars).
- **Water Chemistry:** Magnesium ($Mg^{2+}$) binds to oxygen-rich aroma compounds; Calcium ($Ca^{2+}$) accentuates heavier body; Bicarbonate buffers acidity.
