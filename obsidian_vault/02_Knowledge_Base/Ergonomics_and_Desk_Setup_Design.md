# Ergonomics, Postural Biomechanics & Workspace Design

Sustained intellectual productivity requires an ergonomic workspace that protects spinal biomechanics, minimizes repetitive strain injuries (RSI), and maintains ocular health.

## Neutral Posture Alignment
- **Eye-to-Screen Alignment:** Top third of the primary monitor should be level with resting eye height to maintain a neutral cervical spine. Screen distance should be an arm's length away.
- **Elbows & Forearms:** Desk height should allow forearms to rest parallel to the floor ($90^\circ$ to $100^\circ$ elbow bend) without shrugging shoulders.
- **Feet & Hips:** Feet flat on the floor or footrest; hips slightly higher than knees with a $90^\circ$ to $100^\circ$ knee bend to promote lumbar lordosis.

## Dynamic Workstations
- Alternate between sitting and standing every 60-90 minutes.
- Follow the 20-20-20 rule for eye strain: every 20 minutes, look at an object 20 feet away for at least 20 seconds.
