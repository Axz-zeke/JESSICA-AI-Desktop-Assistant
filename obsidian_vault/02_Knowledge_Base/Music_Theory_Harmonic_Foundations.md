# Music Theory: Harmonic Foundations & Walking Bass Lines

Music theory provides the vocabulary to communicate and navigate chord structures, rhythmic counterpoints, and tonal relationships across the fretboard.

## Walking Bass Construction Rules
1. **Beat 1:** Target the **Root** of the current chord to establish harmonic identity.
2. **Beat 2:** Chord tone (3rd or 5th) or diatonic scale approach.
3. **Beat 3:** Another chord tone or diatonic passing tone.
4. **Beat 4:** Chromatic leading tone a half-step above or below the next chord’s root note.
