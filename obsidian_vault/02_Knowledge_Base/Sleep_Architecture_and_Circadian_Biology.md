# Sleep Architecture, Circadian Rhythms & Recovery

Sleep is an active neurobiological state essential for memory consolidation, hormonal regulation, cellular repair, and glymphatic clearance of metabolic waste in the brain.

## Sleep Stages & 90-Minute Cycles
- **NREM Stage 1 & 2 (Light Sleep):** Transition phase, slowing heart rate and core temperature.
- **NREM Stage 3 (Slow-Wave Deep Sleep):** Delta brainwaves. Peak growth hormone release, physical tissue repair, and immune restoration.
- **REM (Rapid Eye Movement):** Vivid dreaming, emotional regulation, and neural synaptic plasticity.

## Circadian Optimization Protocols
1. **Morning Photons:** View bright sunlight within 30 minutes of waking to trigger cortisol release and set the 16-hour countdown for melatonin synthesis.
2. **Adenosine Clearance:** Adenosine builds sleep pressure throughout waking hours. Caffeine blocks adenosine receptors; avoid caffeine within 8-10 hours of bed.
3. **Thermal Drop:** Core body temperature must drop by 1-2°F to initiate and sustain deep sleep. Keep sleeping quarters cool (~18-20°C).
