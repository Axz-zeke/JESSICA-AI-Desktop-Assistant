# Mechanical Keyboards: Switches, Lubing & Acoustic Modding

A mechanical keyboard is a primary input tactile interface for engineers, programmers, and writers. Acoustic profile and tactile feel can be customized through switch selection and chassis modding.

## Switch Categories
- **Linear (e.g., Gateron Oil Kings, Cherry Red):** Smooth, uninterrupted keypress with no tactile bump. Preferred for gaming and ultra-smooth typing.
- **Tactile (e.g., Holy Panda, Boba U4T):** Subtle to pronounced tactile bump at the actuation point. Ideal for accurate typing.
- **Clicky (e.g., Cherry Blue):** Audible click leaf accompanying the tactile actuation.

## Modding Techniques for Deep "Thock"
1. **Switch Lubing:** Applying Krytox 205g0 to the switch housing rails and stem, and Krytox 105 to the springs, eliminating scratchiness and ping.
2. **Stabilizer Tuning:** Holee mod and dielectric grease / XHT-BDZ on wire ends to eliminate spacebar rattle.
3. **Case Dampening:** Adding poron case foam, tempest tape mod (painters tape on PCB back), and gasket mounting to filter high-pitched clacks into deep acoustic thocks.
