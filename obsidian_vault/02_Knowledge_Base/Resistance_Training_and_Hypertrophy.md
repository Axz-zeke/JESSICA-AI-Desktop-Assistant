# Resistance Training Principles, Hypertrophy & Protein Synthesis

Muscular hypertrophy and physical resilience are physiological adaptations driven by progressive mechanical overload, adequate nutritional substrate, and recovery.

## The Drivers of Muscle Hypertrophy
1. **Mechanical Tension:** The single most potent stimulus. Loading muscle fibers through a full range of motion against high external resistance.
2. **Proximity to Failure (RIR / RPE):** Training sets within 1 to 3 Repetitions in Reserve (RIR) to recruit high-threshold motor units.
3. **Progressive Overload:** Consistently increasing weight, repetitions, or execution control over successive training blocks.

## Nutritional Support
- **Protein Intake:** 1.6 to 2.2 grams of protein per kilogram of body weight per day.
- **Leucine Threshold:** ~2.5g to 3g of leucine per meal to trigger the mTOR pathway and maximize muscle protein synthesis.
