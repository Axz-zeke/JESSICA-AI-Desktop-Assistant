# Deep Work, Attention Capital & Cognitive Flow State

**Deep Work** (coined by Dr. Cal Newport) is the ability to focus without distraction on a cognitively demanding task, optimizing output quality and mental performance.

## The Cost of Attention Residue
When switching between tasks (checking emails, notifications, chat messages), your attention does not switch immediately; a "residue" of attention remains stuck on the prior task. Frequent context switching degrades working memory by up to 40%.

## Protocols for Unbroken Flow
1. **Time Blocking:** Schedule dedicated 90-minute deep work blocks aligned with natural ultradian biological rhythms.
2. **Digital Quarantine:** Close background browser tabs, silence smartphone notifications, and isolate your local coding workspace.
3. **Shutdown Ritual:** End the workday with a clear review of completed tasks and a structured plan for the next morning to eliminate the Zeigarnik effect.
